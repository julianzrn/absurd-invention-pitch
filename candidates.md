# Candidates — One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch

Competition: `u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a`

Brief: one sentence — a pitch for an absurd invention. 8 candidates were drafted in-session; all are original to this entry.

## Candidate 1

Behold the Snooze Leash: it walks your alarm clock around the block until it comes home too tired to ring.

(106 characters)

## Candidate 2 ← SELECTED

Behold the Meeting Moth: a small robot that flies into any meeting running past its agenda and starts eating the minutes until people leave.

(140 characters)

## Candidate 3

Introducing the Apology Vuvuzela: it announces your 'sorry' at stadium volume so nobody can pretend they didn't hear it.

(119 characters)

## Candidate 4

Presenting the Diet Fridge: it gasps theatrically every time you open it after midnight and takes a photo for evidence.

(119 characters)

## Candidate 5

Meet the Procrastination Kettle: it boils water only when you stop watching, then forgets why it started.

(105 characters)

## Candidate 6

Behold the Meeting-Minutes Mime: a silent robot that re-enacts your last meeting until everyone agrees it could've been an email.

(130 characters)

## Candidate 7

Introducing Umbrella Insurance: a drone that follows you holding a tiny umbrella over your ice cream, not you.

(110 characters)

## Candidate 8

Presenting the Compliment Cannon: it fires one sincere compliment per hour at strangers and reloads with gossip.

(111 characters)

## Why the selected candidate wins

The pun does double duty: the moth eats the minutes — the paper record, like a real moth eats paper — and by doing so it ends the meeting, which is the outcome every attendee actually wants. One sentence, a clear picture, and a problem genuinely solved.
